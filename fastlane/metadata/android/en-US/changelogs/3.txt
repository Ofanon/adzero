🎮 AdZero now tells you what it did

When you close a game, a notice sums up your session: how many ads it stopped
while you played, and how many minutes you did not spend watching them. Tap it
for a card you can share.

🏆 Milestones. 100 ads, 1,000, 10,000 — the app celebrates when you get there.

🔧 Something wrong? Two new screens

"A game stopped working" and "An ad got through" now open the last few hours of
activity for that app, with the most likely culprit first and one tap to fix it.
No more guessing which server to touch.

🔄 The blocklist updates itself

New ad servers arrive without waiting for an app update. It can only ever add
servers, never unblock one, and nothing about you is ever sent.

✨ Also

• The app tells you when a new version is out — handy when you installed by hand
• Popups scroll, so long lists are reachable
• Unknown servers are no longer called harmless: AdZero says when it does not know
• Faster: disk writes no longer interrupt the interface
• The icon now shows up on Android 7, where it was missing entirely
